Build a fully functional, production-ready, and high-performance E-Commerce Web Application for a luxury perfume brand called "AURA SCENT".

1. Tech Stack & Architecture
Framework: Next.js (App Router) or React with Vite (TypeScript).

Styling: Tailwind CSS with a luxury Dark/Neutral theme (Deep Obsidian Black #0B0C10, Soft Gold/Champagne #D4AF37, Pure White, and Off-White #F4F4F4).

Animations: Framer Motion for smooth, interactive micro-interactions, layout transitions, float effects for perfume bottles, and entrance animations.

State Management & Persistence: Zustand or React Context with localStorage for cart and wishlist handling.

Icons: Lucide React icons.

2. Core Features & Pages
A. Header & Navigation
Glassmorphism sticky header with blur effect.

Navigation links: Home, Shop, Collections, Fragrance Finder (Quiz), About Us, Contact.

Dynamic Search Bar with live preview/filtering.

Quick action icons: Search, Wishlist (with badge count), Cart (with dynamic counter and slide-over mini-cart), Dark/Light toggle (default to Dark Luxury mode).

B. Hero Section (Home)
High-impact visual layout featuring a highlighted luxury perfume bottle with subtle floating animation.

Compelling typography headline, subheading, and dual Call-to-Action buttons ("Explore Collection", "Take Fragrance Quiz").

Trust badges (e.g., 100% Authentic, Free Express Shipping, Premium Packaging).

C. Product Catalog & Filtering (Shop)
Filter Sidebar / Dropdown: Filter by Scent Family (Woody, Floral, Oriental, Fresh, Citrus), Gender (Men, Women, Unisex), Volume (50ml, 100ml), and Price Range.

Sorting options: Price Low to High, Price High to Low, Best Sellers, New Arrivals.

Interactive Product Cards:

High-quality image with hover zoom/flip effect to secondary image.

Scent Notes preview tag (Top, Heart, Base notes).

Price, Rating (stars), "Add to Cart" button (with instant loading feedback animation), and Quick View modal trigger.

D. Interactive Fragrance Finder Quiz
A 3-step interactive questionnaire guiding users to their ideal perfume based on preference (Occasion, Preferred Season, Intensity).

Instant personalized product recommendations at the end with an "Add Recommended to Cart" option.

E. Product Details Page (PDP) / Modal
Multi-image gallery with thumbnail selection.

Detailed Scent Pyramid breakdown (Top Notes, Heart Notes, Base Notes) displayed visually with icons or badges.

Bottle size selector (e.g., 50ml, 100ml) updating price dynamically.

Interactive Quantity Selector & Direct "Buy Now" checkout button.

Customer Reviews section with average rating summary, verified buyer tags, and a review submission form.

F. Shopping Cart & Mini-Cart
Slide-over drawer for quick cart updates without page reload.

Full Cart page with quantity adjustments, item removal, promo code input, and shipping calculation preview.

G. Checkout Process (UI Simulation)
Clean, multi-step or single-page checkout UI.

Address form with validation, shipping method selection, and mock payment method selection (Credit Card, Apple Pay, Cash on Delivery).

Order Confirmation screen with order summary and animated success checkmark.

3. UX/UI & Aesthetic Standards
Design Vibe: Premium, modern, elegant, and minimal.

Micro-interactions: Smooth hover effects on buttons, interactive active states, ripple effects, and subtle entrance animations using Framer Motion.

Responsive Layout: 100% mobile-friendly and optimized for desktop, tablet, and mobile breakpoints.

Dummy Data: Include a rich set of 8–12 luxury perfume products with realistic names (e.g., "Oud Royale", "Velvet Rose", "Amber Horizon"), detailed fragrance notes, descriptions, ratings, prices, and high-quality Unsplash image URLs.
